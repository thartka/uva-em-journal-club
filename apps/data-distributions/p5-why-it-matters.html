5. Why This Matters

Pathway B really is 1 hour slower than Pathway A, 30 patients in each group. Both tests are looking for that difference. Run 100 studies and count how often each one finds it.

Out of 100 studies, how often did each test find the difference?

t-test parametric: compares means - of 100
Mann-Whitney U non-parametric: compares ranks - of 100

On skewed data the two tests come apart: a handful of boarded patients yank the mean around, so the t-test misses a difference that is really there, while the rank-based test finds it most of the time. On normal data they agree almost perfectly. The test you choose only matters when the data has a shape the test cannot handle, which is why "always use a non-parametric test" is the wrong lesson to take away.
