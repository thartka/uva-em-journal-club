Writing an Abstract

How a structured abstract is built, section by section, using a hyperkalemia study as the running example.

Page 1: The shape of an abstract

Look at four blurred abstracts and pick the ones that are well structured.

Page 2: Background

Two or three sentences: why the problem matters, then the goal.

Page 3: Methods

Study type and data source, criteria, methods of interest, and outcomes.

Page 4: Results

From patients screened to the primary outcome and the chief hypothesis test.

Page 5: Conclusion

One or two sentences that tie directly back to the results.

Page 6: Build an abstract

Place each sentence of a new abstract into the section where it belongs.
