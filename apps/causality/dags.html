4: Confounder and Collider DAGs

← Back to Module

Apply DAG thinking to early antibiotics in sepsis. Click the buttons to see how adjusting for a confounder or conditioning on a collider changes the picture.

Confounder — illness severity

Sicker patients take longer to treat and die more often. Delay and mortality can look linked even if delay does not cause death.

Collider — ICU admission

If you analyze only ICU patients, severity and antibiotic timing become artificially linked through the collider.

Resident reflex: Confounders ask "What else explains both?" Colliders ask "Am I only studying patients who passed a filter?"
